Research in practice.

Three representative papers. For the full list, see Google Scholar ↗

  1. Nature Communications · 2026 · Co-first author

    Machine learning guided formulation design of digital light processing printable elastomers beyond viscosity stretchability tradeoff

    A machine-learning-guided workflow searches resin formulations for DLP-printable elastomers that stay low in viscosity while remaining highly stretchable, demonstrated with a printed compliant gripper.

    Y. Song, B. Park, S. Jeon, H. Kweon, S. Lee, and J. Na

    Paper ↗ Data & code ↗

  2. Carbohydrate Polymers · 2022

    Pore-size control of chitin nanofibrous composite membrane using metal-organic frameworks

    Metal-organic framework (ZIF-8) particles grown within chitin nanofiber membranes adjust the effective pore size, linking membrane composition to size-selective separation.

    Y. Song, J. Y. Seo, H. Kim, S. Cho, and K.-Y. Baek

    Paper ↗

  3. Carbohydrate Polymers · 2019

    Structural control of cellulose nanofibrous composite membrane with metal organic framework (ZIF-8) for highly selective removal of cationic dye

    Combining cellulose nanofibers with ZIF-8 controls the membrane structure and enables highly selective removal of cationic dye from water.

    Y. Song, J. Y. Seo, H. Kim, and K.-Y. Baek

03 / ABOUT

Younghan Song, Ph.D.

My research connects polymer formulation, solution processing, and material architecture.

I study how the composition of a material and the way it is processed decide the structure it forms, and how that structure decides what it can do. The work spans photocurable resins for DLP 3D printing, cellulose and chitin nanofibers organized into films, fibers, and porous membranes, and composites whose woven architectures and interfaces control mechanical performance and selective separation.
