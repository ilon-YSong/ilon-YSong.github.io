Polymer Processing and Materials Design

Younghan Song, Ph.D. · Research Fellow, Konkuk University

I study how resin formulation, solution processing, and fiber architecture shape the properties of polymeric materials. My work focuses on light-cured resins for 3D printing, cellulose-based films and fibers, and woven composites.

Research Areas Contact

01 / RESEARCH

Research Areas

01 / FORMULATEConcept illustration

Photocurable Polymers for 3D Printing

I use rheology, curing measurements, and machine learning to design resins for digital light processing (DLP). A central question is how to keep a resin fluid enough to print while making the cured part stretchable.

DLP · RHEOLOGY · MACHINE LEARNING

02 / ORGANIZEConcept illustration

Cellulose Processing and Nanofibers

I study how solvent composition and flow influence the organization of cellulose in solution and the alignment achieved after regeneration. I also work with cellulose and chitin nanofibers to control pore structure in membranes.

SOLUTION PROCESSING · ALIGNMENT · MEMBRANES

03 / REINFORCEConcept illustration

Woven Fiber Composites

I compare plain and satin carbon-fiber weaves, from how the dry fabric conforms to a shape to how the cured laminate deforms and fails. Testing the fabric and the laminate separately helps distinguish the effects of weave and stacking sequence.

PLAIN & SATIN WEAVES · DRAPING · LAMINATES

03 / ABOUT

About Younghan Song

I am a research fellow in the Department of Materials Engineering at Konkuk University, where I lead a Sejong Science Fellowship project on photocurable resins for 3D printing. I earned my Ph.D. at Konkuk University and held postdoctoral positions at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute and the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST).
